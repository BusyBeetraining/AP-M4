Master the Art of Turning "No" Into "Yes"

Objections are not rejections—they're opportunities. Every objection is a prospect telling you what they need to hear before they say yes. The best appointment setters don't avoid objections; they welcome them because objections mean engagement. A prospect who objects is still listening. Your job is to address their concern, reframe it, and guide them toward booking the appointment.

🎯 Core Truth: The objection you hear is rarely the real objection. "I'm not interested" usually means "You haven't shown me why this matters to me yet." Your goal is to uncover the real concern and address it with confidence and empathy.

The LAER Framework for Handling Objections

Before we dive into specific objections, memorize this framework. Use it for EVERY objection you encounter:

LAER: Your 4-Step Objection Handling System

1️⃣
LISTEN

Let them finish completely. Don't interrupt. Show you're listening: "I understand..."

2️⃣
ACKNOWLEDGE

Validate their concern. "That makes total sense" or "I hear you, that's a common concern."

3️⃣
EXPLORE

Ask a clarifying question. "Can I ask..." or "Just curious..." Understand the root cause.

4️⃣
RESPOND

Address the concern directly and close for the appointment. Always end with a time-specific ask.

⚠️ Never Skip Steps: Jumping straight to your response without listening and acknowledging makes prospects defensive. The LAER framework builds trust and shows you actually care about their concerns.

The 20 Most Common Objections & Proven Responses

These objections cover 90% of what you'll hear. Memorize these responses and customize them to fit your agent's style.

❌ "I'm just browsing online"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"That's how most people start! The thing is, about 70% of the best properties never make it online - they're sold before they hit the market. [Agent Name] has access to those off-market listings. Would a quick 15-minute call work to see if any of those match what you're looking for?"
💡 Why This Works: You validate their online browsing but introduce scarcity (off-market listings). Creates FOMO and positions the agent as having exclusive access to properties they can't find themselves.
❌ "I don't need an agent, I can do it myself"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"I totally respect that! You're right that you CAN do it yourself. The question is - do you have time to handle all the paperwork, negotiations, inspections, and legal requirements? Most people find it's worth having an expert handle that while they focus on their day job. Plus, if you're buying, the seller typically pays the commission anyway. Would you be open to a quick call to see how [Agent Name] could make this easier for you?"
💡 Why This Works: You respect their independence while highlighting the complexity and time commitment. The "seller pays commission" removes the cost objection. You frame the agent as making their life easier, not taking over.
❌ "Take me off your list"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"Absolutely, I can do that right now. Before I do, can I ask what changed? Did you already buy/sell, or is it just not the right time? [Listen to answer] I understand. I'll remove you immediately. Just so you know, if your situation changes down the road, feel free to reach out - we're always here to help."
💡 Why This Works: You comply immediately (shows respect) but ask a qualifying question that might reveal they're still in market with someone else or just annoyed by too many calls. Leaves door open professionally.
❌ "How much does this cost?"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"Great question! There's no cost to you to speak with [Agent Name] - it's a free consultation. If you decide to work together, the commission is typically paid by the [seller/built into the transaction]. [Agent Name] will explain everything on the call. Does [time] work for you?"
💡 Why This Works: You immediately remove the cost barrier by clarifying it's free to talk. You explain commission briefly without getting into complex details (that's for the agent). Then you close for the appointment.
❌ "I'm not pre-approved yet"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"That's actually perfect timing! [Agent Name] works with several great lenders who can get you pre-approved quickly. Would you like [him/her] to introduce you? It's free and takes about 20 minutes. Once you're pre-approved, we can start looking at homes right away! How about we schedule a call with [Agent Name] this week?"
💡 Why This Works: You turn "not pre-approved" into a REASON to talk to the agent (they'll help you get pre-approved). You make it sound quick and easy. This positions your agent as a solution provider, not just a house-shower.
❌ "I can't afford it right now"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"I hear you - affordability is definitely important. That's actually why talking to [Agent Name] early makes sense. [He/She] can help you understand what you CAN afford, show you programs for first-time buyers, down payment assistance, and create a game plan. Would a quick 15-minute call work to explore your options?"
💡 Why This Works: You acknowledge their concern but reframe the agent as someone who helps with affordability (programs, assistance, planning). Many people don't know what they can actually afford or what programs exist.
❌ "I need to save more money first"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"That's smart planning! While you're saving, [Agent Name] can help you understand exactly how much you need and what loan programs might work for you. There are actually programs with as little as 3% down. Would you be open to a quick call to see what your options are? You might be closer to buying than you think!"
💡 Why This Works: You validate their saving goal but introduce the possibility they might not need to save as much as they think. Creates hope and urgency. Positions the agent as an educator who can accelerate their timeline.
❌ "I don't know you / I've never heard of you"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"That's totally fair! That's exactly why I'm calling - to introduce you to [Agent Name]. [He/She] has been serving [area] for [X years] and has helped [X number] of families buy/sell homes. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call just to learn more about what [he/she] offers? No pressure - just information."
💡 Why This Works: You acknowledge their skepticism and use social proof (years in business, number of clients) to build credibility. The "no pressure, just information" lowers their guard.
❌ "I've had bad experiences with agents before"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"I'm really sorry to hear that. Can I ask what happened? [Listen empathetically] That's frustrating. The good news is, not all agents operate that way. [Agent Name] specializes in [communication/responsiveness/transparency] - which is why our clients love working with [him/her]. Would you be willing to give [him/her] a chance with just a quick intro call? If it's not a good fit, no hard feelings."
💡 Why This Works: You listen to their bad experience (builds trust) and position your agent as the opposite of what went wrong. The "no hard feelings" removes pressure and shows you respect their autonomy.
❌ "How did you get my information?"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"Great question! You [filled out a form on our website/requested information about homes in the area/attended an open house/etc.]. I'm following up to see how we can help with your [buying/selling] goals. Is this still something you're interested in?"
💡 Why This Works: You answer directly and honestly, reminding them they expressed interest. This reframes the call from "cold outreach" to "following up on your request." Then you qualify if they're still interested.
❌ "I'm not giving you my information"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"I totally understand - privacy is important. I'm not asking for anything sensitive right now. I just want to see if [Agent Name] can help with your [buying/selling] goals. The only information we'd need is your timeline and what you're looking for. Would a quick 10-minute call work to see if we're a good fit?"
💡 Why This Works: You respect their privacy concern and clarify you're not asking for sensitive data (SSN, financials). You minimize what you need (just timeline and preferences). This lowers their resistance.
❌ "I'm not in that area anymore"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"Oh, no problem! Where are you looking now? [Get new area] Great! [Agent Name] actually has referral partners all over [state/country]. Let me connect you with the right person who specializes in [new area]. Can I get your best number and email?"
💡 Why This Works: Instead of dead-ending the conversation, you pivot to still provide value through a referral. This shows you're helpful (builds trust) and keeps the lead alive through your referral network.
❌ "Stop calling me!"
✅ YOUR RESPONSE:
"I apologize for the inconvenience. I'll remove you from our call list immediately. Can I ask - have you been receiving multiple calls from us? [If yes] I'm sorry about that, there must have been a system error. You won't hear from us again. [If they calm down] Before I go, were you ever interested in [buying/selling], or was the inquiry not from you?"
💡 Why This Works: You de-escalate immediately with an apology and compliance. If they've been called multiple times, acknowledging the error shows accountability. Some angry prospects calm down and become leads if handled with respect.

Advanced Objection Handling Techniques

🔄
The Feel-Felt-Found Method

"I understand how you FEEL. Many of our clients FELT the same way. What they FOUND is that..."

This creates empathy and social proof. "Other people like you had the same concern, but here's what happened when they moved forward."

❓
The Clarifying Question

"Can I ask what specifically concerns you about...?"

Get them talking. Often the stated objection isn't the real one. Asking questions uncovers the true barrier.

🎯
The Boomerang Technique

Turn their objection into a REASON to book.

"I'm too busy" → "That's exactly why this call will save you time!"
"I'm not ready" → "That's the perfect time to get guidance!"

🤝
The Agreement Close

"Makes sense. If I could show you how we solve that exact problem, would you be open to a quick call?"

Acknowledge their concern, then create a conditional agreement. If you solve their problem, will they take action?

⏰
The Urgency Injection

"I totally understand. Just so you know, [Agent Name]'s schedule fills up fast. I have [day/time] available - would that work?"

Creates scarcity. If they wait, they might not get their preferred time slot.

🎁
The Value Stack

List everything they'll get on the call.

"In just 15 minutes you'll get: market analysis, comparable homes, pre-approval guidance, and a customized search setup. Does [time] work?"

What NOT to Do: Common Mistakes

❌ Mistake #1: Arguing with the Objection

Never say "But..." or "Actually..." This makes prospects defensive. Always acknowledge first, then reframe.

❌ Mistake #2: Giving Up Too Easily

Most appointment setters quit after the first objection. Top performers know the first "no" is just a speed bump. Try 2-3 approaches before moving on.

❌ Mistake #3: Talking Too Much

After you respond to an objection, STOP TALKING. Let them respond. Silence is powerful. Don't fill it with nervous chatter.

❌ Mistake #4: Not Using Their Name

Using someone's name builds rapport. "I understand, [Name]..." feels more personal and respectful than "I understand..."

❌ Mistake #5: Forgetting to Close

Every objection response MUST end with an attempt to book the appointment. Don't answer the objection and then wait. Answer, then close.

❌ Mistake #6: Sounding Scripted

These responses are templates, not scripts. Use your own words. Sound natural. Vary your tonality. Authenticity beats perfection.

Bonus: Handling Multiple Objections

Sometimes prospects hit you with multiple objections in one conversation. Here's how to handle it:

🎯 The Stacking Strategy:

When you get multiple objections, address them in order of importance, not order received.

Example:

"I'm too busy, I'm already working with someone, and I don't know if I can afford it."

Your Response:

"I hear you - that's a lot on your plate! Let me address the affordability piece first since that's most important. [Agent Name] can actually help you understand what you CAN afford with today's programs. As for being busy, that's exactly why this 15-minute call will save you time. And regarding your current agent - are you under an exclusive agreement or still exploring options?"

🎯 The Permission Approach:

"I hear three concerns there. Can I address each one? Which is most important to you?"

This shows you're listening, organized, and respectful. Most prospects will tell you their REAL objection when you ask which matters most.
